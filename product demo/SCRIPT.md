# Voiceover Script & Audio Production Guide — assign Product Demo

Total Runtime: 29.5 seconds
Tone: Confident, crisp, authoritative, visionary, modern tech editorial.
Pacing: ~135 words per minute. Punchy, articulate, with clean rests between beats.

---

### [00:00.0 - 00:04.5] Beat 1: Hook & Identity
**Script:**
"Stop pasting tasks into chat windows. Meet **assign**: the Kanban board your AI coding agents work from."

- **Cadence:** Start with an emphatic, grounded punch on *"Stop pasting tasks"*. Slight 0.2s pause after *"chat windows"*. Rise into crisp authority on *"Meet assign"*.
- **Visual Sync:** Typography slams in at 0.4s; LogoMark flares up at 1.8s.
- **SFX:** Cinematic whoosh into deep sub-bass impact, followed by high-register crystal chime.

---

### [00:04.5 - 00:09.5] Beat 2: Multi-Agent Zero Collisions
**Script:**
"Run Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex on one board. With atomic claims, two agents never collide."

- **Cadence:** Rhythmic and confident. Emphasize *"Claude Code, Cursor, and Codex"*. Sharp, definitive delivery on *"atomic claims"* and *"never collide"*.
- **Visual Sync:** Three agent cards stagger in at 4.8s. The atomic lock triggers on task ASG-42 at 6.8s. Branch prefix `nova/asg-42` illuminates at 7.2s.
- **SFX:** Swift swipe entry, key clack, metallic lock click, success confirmation ping.

---

### [00:09.5 - 00:14.5] Beat 3: Live Kanban Engine & Smart Routing
**Script:**
"Watch work move live. Intelligent auto-routing dispatches by skill, load, and priority."

- **Cadence:** Dynamic and flowing. Lean forward on *"Watch work move live"*. Steady, informative cadence on *"skill, load, and priority"*.
- **Visual Sync:** Kanban columns expand at 9.8s. Cards slide seamlessly between Queued, In Progress, and Review. Progress bar fills from 25% to 64% at 11.5s.
- **SFX:** Column glide whoosh, tactile card pop, live event tick, column completion chime.

### [00:14.5 - 00:19.5] Beat 4: AI Assistant & Gemini Live Voice
**Script:**
"Command your workspace with ⌘J, or speak naturally with Gemini Live voice. It plans, routes, and creates tickets while you watch."

- **Cadence:** Modern and effortless. Crisp pronunciation of *"Command J"* and *"Gemini Live"*. Warm conversational tone on *"while you watch"*.
- **Visual Sync:** Keyboard badge pops in at 14.8s. Audio orb pulses with harmonic energy at 15.5s. Live transcript flows as ticket ASG-42 materializes with acceptance criteria.
- **SFX:** Soft shortcut snap, mechanical keyboard typing, ambient vocal resonance, alert sparkle.

---

### [00:19.5 - 00:24.5] Beat 5: By The Numbers — Scale & Security
**Script:**
"Built on serious plumbing: 8 coding tools, 13 MCP primitives, and 6-digit cryptographic pairing."

- **Cadence:** Fast-paced, punchy, escalating momentum. Let each number land like a percussive beat: *"Eight"*, *"Thirteen"*, *"Six-digit"*.
- **Visual Sync:** Dynamic counters roll from 0 to 8 and 0 to 13 at 20.0s. 6-digit OTP verification code `782419` flashes and confirms green at 22.2s.
- **SFX:** Deep bass boom, mechanical counter rolling clicks, digital lock ping, resonant harmonic shimmer.

---

### [00:24.5 - 00:29.5] Beat 6: Grand Finale & Call to Action
**Script:**
"Give your agents a board. Free, self-hosted, and ready in two minutes. Start today at **assign**."

- **Cadence:** Inspiring, triumphant, memorable. Strong final cadence on *"Give your agents a board"* followed by a clear, inviting closing call on *"Start today at assign"*.
- **Visual Sync:** LogoMark and Wordmark burst into center stage with radial iris halo at 25.0s. Feature badges pop into place. Magnetic CTA button illuminates at 26.5s.
- **SFX:** Tension riser at 24.0s culminating in a massive sub-bass impact at 25.0s, fading into a gentle stellar trail.
