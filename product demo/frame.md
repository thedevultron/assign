# Design Spec — assign

The aesthetic is dark, high-contrast, editorial craftsmanship. Flat surfaces with clear tonal elevation steps, hairline borders, precision monospace metadata tags, and glowing Iris and Cyan accents.

## Key Rules
- Headline text: Serif 300 italic highlights (`<em>move.</em>`, `<em>one owner.</em>`).
- Labels and status tags: Roboto Mono, uppercase, letter-spacing +0.08em.
- Cards: Elevation `--s2` (`#161718`) and `--s3` (`#1f2021`) with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Chromatic tokens: Iris `#847dff`, Cyan `#00b3dd`, Orchid `#dd90d8`.
